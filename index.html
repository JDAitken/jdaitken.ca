FAQ

Is there a minimum term?

Yes. Every plan includes a 12-month minimum. After that, you can cancel anytime.

What counts as an edit?

Launch includes small text/photo changes. Growth adds priority edits. Authority includes unlimited reasonable edits within the existing sections and layout.

Do you help with SEO?

Yes. Growth and Authority include local SEO optimization (titles, descriptions, and a Google-friendly structure). Launch covers the essentials.

Can I add pages later?

Absolutely. We can add pages as you grow, or move you to a plan that includes ongoing landing page creation.

What if I already have a site/domain?

No problem. We can migrate your domain and rebuild or refresh your site within the same plan.

What happens after 12 months?

You can keep the plan month-to-month or cancel anytime after the minimum term.
