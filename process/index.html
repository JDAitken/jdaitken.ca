How we work together

Exactly what happens between your first message and your site making the phone ring. Most sites are live in about a week.

01
Day 1

Kickoff call

I start with a kickoff call to understand your business, goals, and customers. Then I review your current site, research competitors, and identify keywords.

  • Kickoff call to understand your business and goals
  • Competitor analysis and keyword research
  • Review current site performance
Deliverable: Project brief and sitemap
02
Days 1-3

Design

I create wireframes and design mockups based on the project brief. You review, provide feedback, and we revise until you're happy.

  • Wireframes and design mockups
  • Review session with feedback
  • Revisions until approved
Deliverable: Final design files
03
Days 3-7

Development

I build your site with clean, performant code. Mobile-first, fast loading, and optimized for search. You get a staging site to review before launch.

  • Build site with clean, performant code
  • Optimize for mobile and speed
  • Integrate forms, SEO, and tracking
Deliverable: Staging site for review
04
Days 7-10

Launch

Final QA, testing across devices, and deployment to production. I set up analytics and monitoring so you can track results from day one.

  • Final QA and cross-device testing
  • Deploy to production
  • Set up analytics and monitoring
Deliverable: Live website
05
Ongoing

Support

I don't build and disappear. Your plan includes post-launch support for bug fixes, minor updates, and monthly check-ins on Premium plans.

  • 30-90 days post-launch support (depending on plan)
  • Monthly check-ins (Premium plan)
  • Bug fixes and minor updates
Deliverable: Monthly report + ongoing updates
