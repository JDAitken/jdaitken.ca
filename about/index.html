Why I started JD Media

A few years ago I was looking at Google Analytics with a trades contractor. His site was pulling 200-300 visitors a month. People searching for exactly what he offered. His phone wasn’t ringing. We pulled up the site on a phone. It took 11 seconds to load. The contact form was broken. The phone number wasn’t clickable.

He’d been paying for a website that was actively turning away customers. He had no idea.

That’s the invisible tax bad web design puts on small businesses. Not a line on the invoice, just calls you never got. Quotes you never sent. Revenue that quietly went to a competitor who had a faster site. I started JD Media to fix that: websites built to get you work, managed by one person who sticks around to make sure they keep working.
