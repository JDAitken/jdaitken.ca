# jdaitken.ca — Product & Execution Plan

## Project Overview
JD Media is a premium web design studio for local service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, etc.). We position as a "Digital Caretaker" — technically excellent, credibility-first, with AI-powered capabilities that future-proof our clients.

## Target Clients
- **Primary**: HVAC companies (Ottawa + surrounding areas)
- **Secondary**: Home service businesses (plumbing, electrical, landscaping)
- **Ideal client size**: 5-50 employees, doing $500K-$5M annually

## Primary Outcomes (In Order)
1. **Credibility**: Position JD as a technical expert, not just another web agency
2. **Inbound leads**: Generate qualified inquiries via portfolio and case studies
3. **Premium pricing**: Command higher rates through demonstrated expertise
4. **Referrals**: Build a reputation that generates word-of-mouth business

## Brand Positioning

### What We Do
Build fast, conversion-optimized websites for local service businesses that show up everywhere (Google, AI search, customer screens).

### How We're Different
1. **Digital Caretaker**: We don't just build and disappear — we monitor, maintain, and improve
2. **AI Integration**: Sites are optimized for AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, etc.)
3. **Technical Excellence**: Fast, accessible, mobile-first — measurable quality
4. **Local Focus**: Deep understanding of Ottawa market and service business needs

### Why It Matters
Most web agencies churn out templated sites. We build custom, technically sound websites that actually generate calls and stay relevant as search evolves.

## AI Integration Strategy

### Positioning (Premium Add-On)
AI features are **not the core service** but a **differentiator** that justifies premium pricing:

**Core Message**: "We build for tomorrow's search landscape, not just today's Google."

### Client-Facing AI Features
1. **AI Search Optimization**: Site structured to appear in ChatGPT, Perplexity results
2. **Automated Monitoring**: AI scans for broken links, SEO issues, opportunities
3. **Lead Intelligence**: AI analyzes lead quality and notifies of high-value prospects
4. **Content Suggestions**: AI recommends blog topics based on search trends

### Implementation Approach
- **Phase 1**: Marketing and positioning only (no actual tools yet)
- **Phase 2**: Simple AI chatbot on JD Media site (demonstrate capability)
- **Phase 3**: Offer AI chatbot to clients as premium add-on
- **Phase 4**: Build custom AI tools (SEO monitor, lead scorer, content generator)

### Messaging Examples
- "Your website shows up when potential customers ask ChatGPT for recommendations"
- "AI tools monitor your site 24/7, alerting you to issues before they cost you leads"
- "We build websites that speak the language of AI search engines"

## Do Not List (Critical)

### Content
- ❌ Fake testimonials or fabricated reviews
- ❌ Stock photos of "diverse business teams"
- ❌ Inflated metrics or made-up case studies
- ❌ Generic marketing fluff ("synergy", "cutting-edge", "revolutionize")

### Design
- ❌ Colors outside tokens-v2.css
- ❌ Heavy animations or unnecessary JavaScript
- ❌ Multiple competing CTAs on same section
- ❌ Dark mode (we're going light for credibility)

### Technical
- ❌ Heavy frameworks (React, Vue) without clear reason
- ❌ Third-party dependencies without vetting
- ❌ Bloated plugins or widgets
- ❌ Forms with >5 required fields

## Implementation Notes

### Tech Stack (Current)
- **Frontend**: Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Hosting**: SiteGround (static hosting with PHP support)
- **Forms**: Formspree (simple, reliable)
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics 4 (to be added)

### Future Considerations
- **Astro**: Static site generator (when content scales)
- **Tailwind**: Utility-first CSS (if component library gets complex)
- **Headless CMS**: For blog/content management (Phase 3+)

### Decision Framework
Before adding new tech:
1. Does it solve a real problem we can't solve with vanilla code?
2. Does it improve performance or just add complexity?
3. Can we maintain it long-term?
4. Does it align with "technically excellent" positioning?
