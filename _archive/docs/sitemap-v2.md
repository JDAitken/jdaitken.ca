## Content Tone & Voice Guidelines

### Do:
- Use "we" and "you" (conversational)
- Lead with benefits, not features
- Use short sentences (10-15 words)
- Be specific: "40% more calls" not "increased engagement"
- Show, don't tell: Use case studies and metrics

### Don't:
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make unsubstantiated claims
- Use aggressive marketing language
- Hide pricing or process details
- Overpromise results
