JD Media (jdaitken.ca), Ottawa, Canada
About: one-person operation run by JD Aitken. JD builds and runs the websites Ottawa trades businesses get their calls from — he does the work himself and clients deal with him directly. Results are measured in calls and quote requests.
Services: managed small-business websites (website + hosting + updates) for a simple monthly fee.
Pricing (12-month minimum term, then month-to-month):

Growth: $500/month (recommended entry plan)
Includes: custom 5-page website (not a template), mobile-first and fast loading, hosting + SSL + domain managed for you, a layout built to turn visitors into callers, Google Business Profile managed monthly (drives map results), monthly SEO content targeting local searches, a simple monthly report on how many people found and called you, up to 3 updates per month, response within 1 business day.
Best for: businesses that want Google to send them customers without paying for ads.

Pro: $900/month
Includes: everything in Growth, Google Ads managed for you (you pay Google directly for ad spend, pause anytime with no penalty), quarterly 15-minute strategy call, same-day response, priority on everything.
Best for: businesses that want their entire online customer acquisition handled for them, including paid ads.

Setup fee: $1,500, waived in full on a 12-month commitment. Applies to Growth and Pro.

Seasonality: the fee is flat year-round and does not change seasonally. Ad spend (Pro) is paid directly to Google and can be paused, raised, or lowered anytime with no effect on the fee. Off-season (roughly December-March) the work shifts rather than stops — SEO and content continue, Google Business Profile posts and review replies continue, plus citation cleanup and spring campaign prep.

Billing options: monthly (default), annual prepay (12 months for the price of 11), or seasonal billing (same 12-month total, weighted heavier April-October and lighter December-March).

Caretaker: $75/month. Not a featured plan — offered directly to existing clients pausing active marketing, e.g. for the off-season, or to anyone who wants the site kept live and safe with no active growth work. Includes hosting/SSL/domain, security updates and backups, GBP kept claimed and monitored, contact forms and call tracking kept working. Does not include new pages, content, reporting, or the ranking guarantee.

Website-only / one-time build: for businesses that just want a site with no ongoing marketing, JD Media also offers one-time project builds, priced as a custom quote. Not a featured plan — ask directly.

Ownership: during the plan, the site is a managed service (hosted and maintained by JD Media). Once a client completes the 12-month minimum, they can request a free static export and take the site with them, anytime, at no charge. Clients are never locked in.

Launch timeline: most sites go live in 7-14 days after content is received.
Guarantee: 90-day money-back guarantee, applies to Growth and Pro. Stay 90 days and if you are not showing up higher in Google local search than when you started, JD Media refunds your management fees for those three months. No fine print.
