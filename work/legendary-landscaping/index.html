Work / Legendary Landscaping
Landscaping Ottawa, ON Website + Local SEO + Google Ads

From a quiet spring to 17 quote requests in a month

Legendary Landscaping builds interlock patios, retaining walls, and hardscape across Ottawa. I rebuilt their site, took over local SEO and their Google Business Profile, then layered in a focused Google Ads campaign. Here's what happened, month by month, from the site relaunch through July 2026.

17 Quote requests in July 2026 Best month since the rebuild. Up from 11 in June and a slow start in April.

The situation

Bradley Laplante built Legendary Landscaping on the strength of the work itself — interlock, retaining walls, hardscape that holds up through Ottawa winters. What the business didn't have was a reliable way for people searching for that work to find it. The old site wasn't built for local search, the Google Business Profile was under-optimized, and there was no paid channel bringing in demand on its own terms.

The goal wasn't a redesign for its own sake. It was quote requests — people who'd already decided they wanted a quote, filling out the form or picking up the phone.

Four months, in order

No single tactic did this. Each month built on the last.

Google Ads launches — and the first month is honest

Ads went live June 15, narrowed to interlock and hardscape only, with three landing pages built specifically for those searches, sitewide conversion tracking, and a rebuilt free-quote form. The first two weeks brought $159 in spend and one lead — a normal cost for a campaign still calibrating. Organic search impressions were already climbing in the background, more than doubling from May.

July 2026

It compounds

Quality Score tuning on the landing pages, duplicate service pages consolidated, the quote form hardened against drop-off. Ad spend nearly doubled to $294 — and cost per lead fell to roughly a quarter of June's, at about $42. Search impressions kept climbing, now over four times May's level, as the site earned visibility for a wider spread of searches.
