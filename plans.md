# jdaitken.ca — Product & UX Plan

## Project Overview
JD Aitken / JD Media is a premium, minimalist web design studio focused on generating calls and leads for HVAC and local service businesses. The site should feel calm, trustworthy, and quietly technical.

## Target Clients
- HVAC companies
- Local home‑service businesses (plumbing, electrical, landscaping, etc.)

## Primary Outcomes
- Inbound leads
- Phone calls
- Quote requests

## Brand Tone & Visuals
- Dark‑mode aesthetic defined by `tokens.css`.
- Calm, premium, minimal, Clay‑inspired energy.
- High contrast, clean typography, generous whitespace.

## Information Architecture Guidance
- Keep core pages distinct and non‑repetitive.
- Clear, linear user path: value → proof → CTA.
- Limit choices to avoid decision paralysis.

## Current Priorities
- CTA clarity and placement.
- Header/nav spacing (mobile‑first).
- Pricing layout clarity.
- Trust signals (Trustpilot integration; real testimonials only).

## Future Roadmap
- AI chatbot integration (lightweight, helpful, non‑intrusive).
- Reusable site templates / lead magnets.
- SEO automation for local service keywords.

## Do Not List
- No fake testimonials or fabricated reviews.
- No new colors outside `tokens.css`.
- No bloated animations or unnecessary plugins.
- No heavy libraries unless explicitly approved.

## Implementation Notes
- Keep changes small and safe.
- Prefer vanilla HTML/CSS/JS.
- Forms should be reliable for SiteGround static hosting with simple PHP handlers.
