Why web design?

I started building websites and quickly realized most service businesses were getting a raw deal. Agencies churn out templated sites, charge a premium, then disappear. The sites look fine at first, but they're slow, don't convert, and fall apart on mobile. I wanted to do better.

Why service businesses?

Service businesses are the backbone of local economies. The HVAC tech, the plumber, the electrician — they deserve websites that work as hard as they do. Not outdated templates that cost them leads every day.

Why Ottawa?

I'm based in Ottawa and love working with local businesses. I understand the market, the competition, and what it takes to stand out in a city where homeowners search online before they call.

What I bring to the table

Mobile-first design

Every site starts at 375px and scales up. Your customers are on their phones — the site needs to work perfectly there first.

Conversion optimization

Clear CTAs, trust signals, and friction-free forms. Every design decision is made to generate more calls and leads.

Local SEO

Structured data, optimized titles, service area pages. Your site shows up when Ottawa homeowners search for your services.

Technical excellence

Clean code, fast load times, accessible markup. Built to score 90+ on Lighthouse across all categories.
