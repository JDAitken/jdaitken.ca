How we work together

A transparent, collaborative process — from discovery to launch to ongoing support.

01
Week 1

Discovery

We start with a kickoff call to understand your business, goals, and customers. We review your current site, research competitors, and identify keywords.

  • Kickoff call to understand your business and goals
  • Competitor analysis and keyword research
  • Review current site performance
Deliverable: Project brief and sitemap
02
Week 2

Design

We create wireframes and design mockups based on the project brief. You review, provide feedback, and we revise until you're happy.

  • Wireframes and design mockups
  • Review session with feedback
  • Revisions until approved
Deliverable: Final design files
03
Weeks 3–4

Development

We build your site with clean, performant code. Mobile-first, fast loading, and optimized for search. You get a staging site to review before launch.

  • Build site with clean, performant code
  • Optimize for mobile and speed
  • Integrate forms, SEO, and tracking
Deliverable: Staging site for review
04
Week 5

Launch

Final QA, testing across devices, and deployment to production. We set up analytics and monitoring so you can track results from day one.

  • Final QA and cross-device testing
  • Deploy to production
  • Set up analytics and monitoring
Deliverable: Live website
05
Ongoing

Support

We don't build and disappear. Your plan includes post-launch support for bug fixes, minor updates, and monthly check-ins on Premium plans.

  • 30–90 days post-launch support (depending on plan)
  • Monthly check-ins (Premium plan)
  • Bug fixes and minor updates
Deliverable: Peace of mind

What we need from you

A few things to have ready so we can hit the ground running.

Brand assets

Logo, colors, fonts — anything that represents your business visually.

Content

Service descriptions, about text, and photos. We can help write if needed.

Hosting / domain access

Login credentials for your hosting provider and domain registrar.

Timely feedback

Quick turnaround on design reviews keeps the project on schedule.
