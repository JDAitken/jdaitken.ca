Usage Guidelines

Do's

  • Use Deep Navy (#1A2332) as the primary brand color across all materials
  • Reserve Warm Coral (#FF6B4A) for calls-to-action and key accent elements
  • Maintain generous white space for clarity and professionalism
  • Keep typography clean and hierarchical
  • Prioritize readability and accessibility in all designs

Don'ts

  • Don't overuse the coral accent—less is more
  • Avoid cluttered layouts or excessive visual elements
  • Don't use gradients except for subtle hero backgrounds
  • Avoid stock photos that feel generic—prefer custom graphics or authentic client photos
  • Don't mix additional colors outside the established palette
